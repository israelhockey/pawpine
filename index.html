How the guarantee works

Questions

What kind of photo works best?+

A clear phone photo where your pet's face is in focus and well lit. Looking at the camera is great, but sleepy faces and side angles work too.

Is the preview really free?+

Yes. Upload a photo, try every scene, and only pay if you love it. Previews carry a small watermark; your order is printed without it.

Will it arrive before Christmas?+

Order by December 11 for standard delivery before Christmas in the US, or by December 16 with express shipping. Digital downloads arrive by email in minutes, right up to Christmas morning.

Can I put two pets in one portrait?+

Not in the online maker yet. Email us a photo with both pets together and we'll make it for you.

What if I don't love it?+

You approve your portrait before anything is printed. If it arrives and it isn't right, tell us within 30 days and we'll redo it or refund you.
