Christmas gifts for dog lovers they'll actually keep

Thoughtful Christmas gift ideas for dog moms, dog dads and anyone whose dog runs the house, from personalized portraits to small stocking stuffers.

Buying for a dog lover is easy in one way: you already know what they care about most. The hard part is finding something better than another bag of treats. The gifts that land are the ones that feel like they were made for their dog, not just any dog.

1. A portrait of their dog

Most dog owners have hundreds of photos on their phone and none on the wall. A painted portrait turns their favorite photo into something they'll see every day. Choose a style that fits their home: a classic oil look for a living room, or something festive they can bring out every December.

Tip: borrow a photo from their social media. A clear shot of the dog's face works best.

2. A personalized ornament

An ornament with their dog's face on it becomes part of the tree every year. It's also a good choice if you want something small for a coworker, neighbor or dog walker.

3. A blanket for the couch they share

Most dogs end up on the sofa eventually. A soft throw with their portrait on it is cozy, a little funny, and gets used all winter.

4. Stocking stuffers

  • A mug with their dog's portrait for morning coffee
  • A new toy or long-lasting chew for the dog
  • A framed print of a favorite photo for their desk
